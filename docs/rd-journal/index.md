# R&D journal

The R&D journal records the experiments carried out on the project, **including those that did not work**: knowing why an approach failed avoids exploring it again.

## Adding an entry

1. Copy the [template](template.md) to `docs/rd-journal/YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md`.
2. Fill in the sections; the outcome is one of: **Works**, **Partially works**, **Does not work**.
3. Add the page to the `R&D journal` section of `nav` in `zensical.toml`, and to the list below.
